# AI Cost Modeling

Use this skill for tasks involving model pricing, token budgets, or AI feature operating cost.

## Focus

- identify cost drivers
- estimate per-request and monthly cost
- separate prompt, completion, embedding, and tool/runtime cost
- recommend guardrails such as caching, truncation, and model tiering
